# Results: buying after a detected bundle snipe

Every number below is regenerated by the scripts in `code/` from the frozen snapshot in `data/`. The
tables they write are in `tables/`; each section names its table. Rates are complete-case (only price
windows the detector actually covered, see [methodology.md](methodology.md) §4), with a Wilson 95 %
interval and the denominator next to them. "P0" is the spot price the detector read at the alert, not
an execution price. An MFE is a maximum that was touched, not a price that was cashed.

**Short version.** Among 1,657 bundle snipes detected live between 2026-08-21 and 2026-09-29, a buyer
entering at the alert price saw at least 2x within one hour in 36.3 % of covered cases, 30.1 % within
15 minutes, 1.5 % within 60 seconds, and 0 of 1,445 within 10 seconds. Of 12 signals locked before the
test, 11 did not survive it, including every signal for the 1-hour target and every rule that reached
80 % in training. One survived: a logistic score raises P(2x within 15 min) from 31.0 % to 45.7 %
(N = 164) on the held-out test. It still loses money. Holding for 2x, the trades that miss lose 99 % on
average, which puts break-even at 49.7 % before costs and 58.5 % after.

On the primary target, **0 of 37 features** pass Benjamini-Hochberg in TRAIN. Across the full grid
(every feature against every threshold and horizon with at least 5 TRAIN positives), 58 of 416 AUCs
pass it. 57 keep their direction in VALIDATION, and they concentrate at 5 and 15 minutes and at 1.5x
and 2x. The information exists, but it is about how fast a doubling comes, not whether it comes.

## 5. Searching for a pattern in TRAIN (T3)

Three targets, fixed before the search: 2x within 5 min, 15 min and 1 h (1 h primary). For each:
1,461 conditions in total (every feature against its TRAIN deciles, every category), 482,519
conjunctions of up to three conditions evaluated, a ridge logistic regression and a shallow tree.

### Why a 45.7 % doubling rate loses money (T8)

| Strategy on TEST, +10 s | trades | reached TP | mean of the others, gross | break-even hit rate, gross | break-even, net ($500) | gross mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| every alert, hold to 2x for 1 h | 526 | 36.9 % | -95.5 % | 48.9 % | 57.0 % | -23.4 % |
| survivor, hold to 2x for 15 min | 163 | 44.8 % | -98.9 % | 49.7 % | 58.5 % | -9.8 % |
| survivor, TP 2x / SL -20 % / 5 min | 169 | 22.5 % | -23.0 % | 18.7 % | 32.1 % | +4.6 % [-9.1, +14.6] |

A launch that does not double within 15 minutes has, on average, already lost nearly everything (-98.9 %). Holding
for 2x therefore needs more than one success in two just to break even before costs. With a stop, the
losers are cut and break-even drops to 18.7 %, but the stop also cuts the doublings (22.5 % instead of
44.8 %), and the estimated costs take the rest: 11 to 15 points per round trip at $500 (about 5 at $100),
which is what fees and price impact cost in a pool holding about 70 SOL.

# FINAL VERDICT

> **Can one detect a bundle snipe, then buy, and get 2x in a proportion high enough and reproducible
> enough to call it an edge?**

No. Doublings are common, since about a third of all alerts reach 2x within an hour from the alert
price. One pre-entry signal does shift that probability reproducibly. But no rule known before entry
reaches a high probability of 2x out of sample, and the one that survives does not turn into money.

```text
BASE RATE:   P(2x within 15 min) = 31.0 % (188/606, TEST); within 1 h = 37.9 % (200/528, TEST)
SIGNAL:      ridge logistic score >= its TRAIN 80th percentile (cheap entry relative to the
             operator's price, single-transaction actor, fast alert)
P(2x):       TRAIN 49.1 % (N 55), VALIDATION 35.6 % (N 177)
N:           396 alerts selected over the three splits
TEST P(2x):  45.7 % [38.3, 53.4] within 15 min
N TEST:      164
LIFT:        +14.7 pts against the base, +20.2 pts against the rest [+10.7, +30.3]
LATENCE:     the detector alerts 11.8 s after creation; +0 to +20 s after the alert moves the
             hold-to-2x gross result from -8.0 % to -14.0 %
SLIPPAGE:    estimated, depth derived from price: net -15.3 % at $100, -23.3 % at $500,
             -32.0 % at $1,000 per trade
EDGE NET:    -23.3 % per trade [-35.9, -12.0]; break-even would need 58.5 % of doublings,
             and no rule reaches it out of sample
```

**SIGNAL STATISTIQUE MAIS PAS D'EDGE TRADABLE**

For the two questions of the study:

1. Buying immediately after detection still yields 2x in 36.3 % of cases within an hour (0 % within
   10 s, 1.5 % within 60 s), but under every pre-declared strategy tested the average trade loses,
   before costs (holding) or after them (stops).
2. A pattern observable at detection exists. It moves P(2x within 15 min) from 31.0 % to 45.7 % and
   survives the test, clustering by operator and the August/September split. It does not come near a
   high probability: every rule that reached 70 to 80 % in training returned to the base rate, and at
   1 h nothing beats the base rate at all.
