# Plan: which wallets are the author's, and how sure

Written and committed before any profit is read by attribution level. Results stay in the private folder.

A wallet sending to a deposit address is not proof of ownership on its own: anyone can pay an exchange deposit address, and an exchange hot wallet pays
all its customers. So no list of senders is ever taken as his wallets. Each wallet gets one level, from this rule, with its proofs listed.

**CERTAIN.** It sends to the deposit address he declared, and one of his own PnL cards matches one of its round trips on the token symbol and on all of:
invested within 2 %, gain within 2 %, holding time within 60 s (cards without figures: percentage within 1 % and holding time). This rests on his statement
that the address and the cards are his. Nothing on chain names a person.

**PROBABLE.** It sends to the deposit address, and at least one of:
- a direct transfer of tokens, or of at least 0.01 SOL, with a CERTAIN wallet or with another wallet already PROBABLE, applied until nothing changes;
- two or more returns from the exchange hot wallet, and at least half of its trades through the same tool as the cards (the fee account of the tool in the
  same transaction).

**NOT_ESTABLISHED.** Everything else that touched the perimeter: a wallet that only sends to the deposit address, and every wallet outside it, however it
is linked. Its trades are listed and never added to a total of his.

**EXCLUDED_INFRASTRUCTURE.** More than 60,000 signatures, the rule of the frozen study: exchange-like, not a person's trading wallet.

Limits written in advance. A level says how the chain and his statements fit together, not who held a key. A shared service, a shared bot wallet or a gift
could produce any of these links, and the hot-wallet branch of PROBABLE is the weakest. Wallets that never paid the deposit address and never exchanged
with the perimeter stay invisible.
