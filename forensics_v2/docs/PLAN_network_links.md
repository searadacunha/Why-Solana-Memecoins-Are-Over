## How each wallet is labelled

Fixed in advance, on any page read.

- **`service-like`:** at least 18 distinct senders of at least 0.01 SOL in 100 transactions, the lower figure seen on the hub in `reports/fleet_structure`, or more than 200 transactions in an hour.
- **`private-like`:** at most 5 distinct senders of at least 0.01 SOL in 100 transactions, and at least 80 % of SOL received from them.
- **`undetermined`:** anything else, and any wallet with a public service label is `labelled`, whatever it does.

## How the bursts are read

- **One network, beyond the 3 bursts:** at least half of the other senders to `6aRTeY51…`, by count, have a first transaction that is a direct credit from the gateway, and `6aRTeY51…` is not `service-like`. That means the same sweep serves gateway-funded wallets that are not in these bursts.
- **A service or a shared sweep:** `6aRTeY51…` is `service-like`, or under a quarter of its other senders are gateway-funded, and they mostly came from elsewhere.
- **Otherwise `UNRESOLVED`.**
- **Common purchases across the 3 bursts:** the mints bought by a member of two different bursts within the same hour, with the share of each burst's mints this makes. It is the plan's test of a coordinated group, and is descriptive. Burst 138, the same day and without a join, is read as a negative control of that count, 3 members only, if they are cached.
- **The way back to the gateway:** each direct transfer to the gateway from the wallets on these paths, with amount and time. A gateway credit alone is not a link.

## For the three destinations

A destination joins two groups only if it is not `service-like` and not `labelled`, and it, or a wallet it pays directly, is on the path of another group. Nothing else counts as a join. A `private-like` label says only that few wallets pay it, not who holds it.

## Limits

Short pages. A label applies to the moments read, and a service may look private on a quiet day. No label means no label, not no owner. Nothing here identifies a person.
