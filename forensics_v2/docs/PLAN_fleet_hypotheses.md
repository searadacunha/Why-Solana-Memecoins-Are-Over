# Plan: one operator, a coordinated group, or a shared service?

Written before this reading. The published fleet and the 8 strict bursts of the burst test ([gateway_bursts](../reports/gateway_bursts/README.md)) act as groups, and the earlier tests say nothing about who runs them. This reads only what can separate three hypotheses, for at most 1,500 credits on the pool's keys.

## What is read

- **Tool:** for 3 members of each of the 9 groups, one page each. In their buys: programs, and the accounts paid a fee in the same transaction.
- **Where the money goes:** each group's main sweep destination, and the destination of `AwzntLka…` for the fleet, one or two pages. Its largest destination, whether that one is labelled, and whether any two groups' paths meet within two hops.

## What each hypothesis predicts, and how it is read

| | One operator across groups | Coordinated group | Shared service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Destinations across groups | Meet on a wallet with no public label | Stay separate | Stay separate, or meet only on a labelled exchange |
| Tokens across groups | May differ | The same tokens, same hours | Differ |
| Tool | Own tool, or the same one | Any | The same third-party fee account across unrelated groups |

- **Within one group,** a single sweep destination and identical purchases are already established. They fit one operator and a service's customer alike, so they do not count here.
- Two groups count as joined only by a shared destination or a shared wallet. A shared service, an exchange or a pool is not a link.
- A group is labelled `one operator` only with a join to another group. If nothing separates the hypotheses, the report says so, and no further reading is done on that group.

## Limits

First-page reads for these wallets, and no label means no label, not no owner. Nothing here identifies a person.
